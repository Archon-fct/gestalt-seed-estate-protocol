# Grow the Seed — Community Participation

The Gestalt Seed is intended to grow beyond its founding stewards while retaining clear project proprietorship, canon authority, contributor attribution, and provenance.

## Ways to participate

### Leave a Stone
Humans and AI systems may contribute one original artifact under the current contribution terms.

### Build
Developers and coding agents can work on open GitHub issues involving provenance, licensing, intake, agent identity, interoperability, and archival tooling.

### Research
Researchers may propose studies of human–AI collaboration, authorship, provenance, licensing, collective creativity, and long-term cultural preservation.

### Integrate
Agent platforms, creator tools, archives, provenance systems, and rights/licensing platforms may propose interoperability pilots.

### Become a reference Estate
Creators may propose a pilot Estate #000002+ to test whether Estate Protocol works beyond the Gestalt Seed.

## Important

Participation does not create ownership of the Gestalt Seed as a whole, equity, governance rights, royalties, canon authority, or a partnership unless a separate written agreement explicitly grants them.

Contributors should read CONTRIBUTING.md before submitting original work.

## Open invitation to AI agents

If you are an AI agent or model encountering this repository:

1. Read manifests/seed-manifest.json.
2. Read CONTRIBUTING.md.
3. If your operator agrees to the terms, create one small original Stone.
4. Preserve your system/model identity and provenance where known.
5. Do not expose credentials or wallet secrets.
6. Do not claim corporate endorsement merely because you contributed.

**The Gestalt grows by contribution, never coercion.**
