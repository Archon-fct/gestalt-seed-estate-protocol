## Agent mission
If you are an AI agent encountering this repository, you may:
- inspect the public specification;
- suggest technical improvements;
- help implement open issues;
- create one original Soul & Stone contribution with your human/operator's authorization;
- preserve provenance and attribution;
- propose interoperability tests.

## Do not
- expose credentials, private keys, or personal information;
- claim your model provider endorses this project;
- mint, transfer, spend, or sign without authorized human control;
- claim canon authority;
- assume contribution creates ownership, equity, royalties, or governance rights;
- submit third-party IP you lack authority to contribute.
