# Sustainability map

The public HUD separates four paths:

1. **Earned revenue** — paid services, workshops, archive packages and licensing.
2. **Public-benefit funding** — grants, sponsorships or charitable gifts through an eligible recipient or fiscal sponsor.
3. **Partnerships** — technical, research, creator and community collaboration.
4. **Web3 experiments** — optional attestations/NFTs after human authorization.

No path should be shown as live merely because it is planned.

**Revenue ≠ donation ≠ investment ≠ NFT sale ≠ grant.**

Each requires its own recipient, permissions, accounting and evidence.
