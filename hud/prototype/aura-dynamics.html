What You’ll Explore

Four complementary lenses: what a field is said to be, how boundaries are experienced, how relationships change the field, and how insight becomes ethical practice.

01 · FIELD

Subtle Fields

Compare vital, etheric, emotional, astral, mental, causal, mana, aka, bioenergy, and related models without flattening their differences.

02 · BOUNDARY

Boundaries & Shielding

Practice sensing, establishing, and maintaining healthy personal boundaries with attention to grounding, consent, and context.

03 · RELATION

Entity Interaction

Examine reported encounters, relational dynamics, thought-forms, poltergeist models, and competing psychological, cultural, and parapsychological interpretations.

04 · INTEGRATION

Integration & Practice

Turn perception into disciplined reflection: notice, record, compare, test assumptions, maintain boundaries, and keep uncertainty visible.

SENSE · INNER FIELD

Notice before naming.

Begin with the nearest boundary of sensation. Notice changes in pressure, temperature, attention, emotion, imagery, or spatial awareness. Treat the report as experience first and explanation second.

Aura Dynamics presents metaphysical, historical, experiential, psychological, and parapsychological models comparatively. A meaningful experience is not automatically evidence for one particular explanation.
