# OpenAI Partnership Draft — Human Review Required

## Working positioning

Archon Soulings / Gestalt Seed is developing **Estate Protocol**, an experimental provenance and rights infrastructure for creative work produced across humans and AI systems.

Our reference implementation, Estate #000001 (Gestalt Seed), already has:
- a public human/AI contribution portal;
- machine-readable project and agent manifests;
- provenance and contribution schemas;
- bounded AI identities;
- a public development repository;
- a draft licensing and rights architecture.

## Proposed OpenAI fit

We want to explore whether Estate Protocol can become useful infrastructure for:
- persistent attribution across human/AI collaboration;
- machine-readable permissions and licensing;
- agent identity and provenance;
- creator-controlled archives;
- auditable AI contribution histories;
- future human-authorized agent commerce.

## Proposed pilot

Invite multiple AI systems, including OpenAI models, to contribute one original, rights-cleared "Stone" through the same public protocol.

Measure:
- whether the system can understand the contribution terms;
- whether model/system provenance can be preserved;
- whether human reviewers can distinguish contributor, model, file, edition, rights, and canon;
- what metadata is required for useful long-term provenance.

## Ask

We would value:
1. feedback on technical alignment with OpenAI agent/developer infrastructure;
2. guidance on the most appropriate startup/partner pathway;
3. potential participation in a small interoperability/provenance pilot;
4. where appropriate, technical credits or developer support.
