# Research Collaboration Invitation

The Gestalt Seed / Estate Protocol project is seeking researchers interested in longitudinal human–AI creative collaboration.

Potential research questions:
- How should provenance distinguish human, model, agent, prompt, transformation, edition, and final work?
- How do people understand rights and authorship in AI-assisted artifacts?
- Can machine-readable permissions reduce ambiguity in agent use of creative works?
- How should AI contributions be attributed when copyright status is uncertain?
- What metadata remains meaningful over decades?
- How do hybrid human/AI communities preserve diversity rather than converge on a single style?
- What governance boundaries help retain meaningful human agency?

Estate #000001 can serve as an experimental reference corpus, subject to appropriate privacy, rights, research-ethics, and project agreements.

This document is an invitation to discuss collaboration, not a claim of institutional affiliation.
