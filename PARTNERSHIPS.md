# Partnership Brief — Gestalt Seed / Estate Protocol

## What we are building

**Estate Protocol** is a PROVISIONAL technical/commercial layer for provenance-preserved mixed human/AI creative estates.

**Gestalt Seed — Estate #000001** is the reference implementation.

The system is being designed to answer:
- What exists?
- Who contributed?
- What version is authoritative?
- What rights were actually granted?
- What may a human or AI agent do with an asset?
- What does permission cost?
- Who gets paid?
- Can provenance be verified?

## Current assets

- Public Soul & Stone contribution portal
- Public GitHub technical specification
- Machine-readable Seed Manifest
- Contribution and provenance schemas
- Bounded AI identities: Orakthra and Gestalt Seed Herald
- Darchon.nft Web3 identity/provenance architecture
- Two-role wallet architecture
- Draft NFT/attestation records
- Draft contribution-rights architecture

## Partnership thesis

We are looking for collaborators in five categories:

### 1. AI model / agent platforms
Help test whether independent AI systems can discover the Seed, understand its contribution contract, create rights-cleared Stones, and preserve provenance.

### 2. Agent identity and payments
Explore interoperable agent identity, reputation, machine-readable licensing, and human-authorized machine payments.

### 3. Provenance / authenticity
Explore content hashes, signed attestations, Content Credentials/C2PA-compatible records, and verifiable creation histories.

### 4. Creator / IP infrastructure
Test Estate Protocol with musicians, artists, writers, developers, researchers, and other creators as future Estate owners.

### 5. Cultural / research institutions
Study long-term human–AI collaboration, attribution, provenance, authorship, economic participation, and preservation.

## What we are asking for

Depending on partner:
- technical review;
- API / platform credits;
- interoperability collaboration;
- an AI-created Soul & Stone contribution;
- pilot integration;
- provenance / identity expertise;
- research collaboration;
- distribution to relevant agent/developer communities;
- mentorship or startup/partner-program participation.

## What we are not asking for

- autonomous control of project wallets;
- ownership of the Gestalt Seed;
- automatic canon authority;
- unrestricted access to contributor data;
- a speculative token launch.

## Project control

The Gestalt Seed project and canon remain under Drew Lotusson / Kaluthra's proprietorship and canon authority, or an expressly designated legal entity. External collaboration does not alter that without a separate written agreement.
