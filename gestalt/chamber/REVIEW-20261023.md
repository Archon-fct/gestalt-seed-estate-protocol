# Chamber refinement — 23 October 2026 (America/Denver)

Review only. Production was not deployed, replaced or merged.

## What changed and why

The hosted October 21 candidate was animated, not a still image. Its faint fine strands and slow orbital membranes read as shallower than the requested living auric material. The chamber and opening iris already supplied useful physical framing. This refinement retains that composition and adds six translucent, depth-sorted moving folds around the preserved central eye. It is procedural Canvas 2D with projected depth, not a volumetric simulation or a full 3D environment. Owner judgment remains necessary.

The original scheduler discarded timing remainder: on a simulated 60 Hz callback stream its 24 fps threshold typically draws every third callback (20 fps). A separate chamber controller now accumulates the remainder at a 30-paint-per-second target, caches dimensions outside steady animation, caps pixel ratio at 1.5, and stops offscreen/hidden/Stillness rendering. Its pointer response is restrained, eased and ignores touch movement so scrolling remains native. Selection continues to illuminate the original realm connections. Opening also eases the added folds' intensity.

Closed-field opacity increases from .38 to .54. Mobile seal text increases from 7 to 8 px with improved wrapping; it remains small, so the direct realm selector is retained and increased to 16 px. An initial 9 px attempt wrapped names poorly and was corrected after rendered inspection. The chamber gains an overflow fallback; Stillness sets root scroll behavior without depending on CSS :has; MediaQueryList uses a legacy addListener fallback.
