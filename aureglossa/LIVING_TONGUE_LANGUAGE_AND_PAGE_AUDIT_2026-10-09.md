## 1. Existing page assessment

The earlier portal provided six interactive concept nodes: Tharavel, Kosmathra, -thra, ar, sei, and the preserved chant. It had a provenance reader, a return link to the Nexus, opt-in Atmosphere, Stillness, and browser-session Passport marks. Its principal limitations were **scope and teachability**: it presented six examples but not the grammar system, full recorded pronoun/particle inventory, source-language policy, or historical layers. The geometric glyph ring could imply an alphabet without actually displaying canonical glyphs. No alphabet or phonetic assets were included. The motto warning was substantially accurate and must remain.

## 2. Recovered linguistic authority

- Productive grammar: **Subject – Aspect – Verb – Object – Relation**. Person-uninflected verbs; unmarked declarative timeless/general absent context; identity copula omission permitted; possession **possessed + ar + possessor**; negation directly before negated expression.
- Nine pronouns: **na, tha, el, it, nar, nam, thar, elar, esh**.
- Twenty-six particles: aspect **sha, dur, et, ir, re**; relation **ar, ta, va, en, ath, ka, or, ul, sur**; logic **ae, au, dar, thal, vara, nu, ra**; ritual **o, ha, sei, vek, amen**.
- Source-language policy: securely ancient Sanskrit, Classical Latin, Classical Ancient Greek, Sumerian, Gothic. Individual roots still need historically attested forms/citations; source-language influence is not etymological proof.
- Mantle suffix **-thra**: bearer/embodiment/entrusted mantle; normally unstressed. Bound roots **kalu-, orak-, vasan-** must not be promoted to independent words.
- **THARAVEL**: adopted atomic verbal/imperative-capable form; no invented decomposition into thara + vel; historical etymology provisional. **KOSMATHRA**: productive mantle morphology; kosma- Greek-inspired, exact historical citation pending. English motto is poetic, not word-for-word.
- Legacy collisions: **na** and **et** have older functions. Preserve attested older texts and their editions rather than silently rewriting to the current productive grammar.
- The ten-line Sung Aureglossa Chant is **attested**, not fully translated; **24 forms remain unresolved**.
- Master has a **32-letter phonetic inventory**, but the original exact phonetic/glyph assets are not in this page; no IPA, glyph alphabet, or audio pronunciation is invented.
- A legacy **827-entry** layer is described in the recovered handoff. It was **not** exhaustively re-audited entry by entry in this task.

## 3. New page behavior (feature branch)

The existing interactive six-form environment remains. An accessible **Explore the Living Language** dialog adds five structured chapters:

1. **Origins** — five source strata; current productive versus historical/legacy layer; honest alphabet-asset boundary.
2. **Grammar** — five-part syntax architecture, uninflected verbs, possession, negation, invitation/command and a clearly marked **structural placeholder** rather than an invented sentence.
3. **Lexicon** — all nine documented pronouns, all twenty-six documented particles, -thra and three attested mantle roles.
4. **Motto** — the approved inscription and English project gloss, with the two unresolved etymology warnings.
5. **Archive** — the attested chant's endpoints and unresolved status, edition separation and a source-audited path for future vocabulary.

The chapter UI uses an actual native `<dialog>`, keyboard-operable tabs, focus visibility, responsive reading layout and screen-reader status. The original six nodes, provenance gesture, Passport marks, Stillness, opt-in Atmosphere and independent Nexus return remain in place. The new atlas performs **no network calls, storage writes, analytics, microphone recording, or private-data import**.
