# Aureglossa Linguistic Audit — THARAVEL KOSMATHRA

Audit date: 2026-10-06
Status after audit: **ADOPTED MOTTO; lexical derivation partially provisional**
Display: **THARAVEL · KOSMATHRA**
Intended English: **Go further · Live passionately · Build the soul of the cosmos**

## Audit basis

Checked against the recovered Aureglossa Canonical Master v1.0 rules summarized in the collected-works handoff:
- productive grammar is Subject–Aspect–Verb–Object–Relation;
- verbs do not inflect for person;
- bare verbs can serve as neutral instructions in imperative context;
- new lexical sources are restricted to Vedic/ancient Sanskrit, Classical Latin, Classical Ancient Greek, Sumerian, and Gothic;
- unsupported derivations must be labeled inspired;
- `-thra` is already productive mantle morphology: “one who bears, embodies, or is entrusted with a mantle.”

Also checked known earlier Aureglossa usage preserved in project conversation:
- `thara` has previously been used with the gloss **center**;
- `vel` has previously been used with the gloss **steady**;
- therefore the earlier proposed decomposition “thara = beyond” + “vel = passionate forward life” conflicts with prior usage and is rejected.

## Findings

### THARAVEL

**Surface form:** Tharavel
**Intended role in motto:** verbal/imperative complex: go further; live forward with passionate vitality.

**Collision:** Earlier project usage gives `thara` = center and `vel` = steady. The phrase therefore cannot honestly be canonized using the previously proposed root story.

**Resolution:** Preserve **Tharavel** as an adopted whole lexical form whose meaning is fixed by project use, but do not currently segment it as `thara + vel`.

Recommended lexical status:
- lemma: `tharavel`
- class: verb / ritual imperative-capable lexical item
- meaning: **to go beyond one's present limit through vivid, committed living; to advance with whole-hearted vitality**
- imperative gloss: **Go further. Live passionately.**
- derivation: **Aureglossa internal coinage; historical etymology unresolved**
- canon status: **ADOPTED, ETYMOLOGY PROVISIONAL**
- pronunciation: must be reconciled with the canonical 32-letter phonetic inventory before an IPA form is declared. Do not infer it solely from English spelling.

This avoids corrupting the older `thara` and `vel` records.

### KOSMATHRA

**Surface form:** Kosmathra
**Intended role:** “the bearer/embodiment/steward of the cosmic mantle,” poetically “the soul of the cosmos.”

**Confirmed morphology:** `-thra` already means one who bears, embodies, or is entrusted with a mantle. Therefore `-thra` should NOT be glossed simply as “soul” or “living essence.” The earlier explanation is corrected.

**Root candidate:** `kosma-`
Classical Ancient Greek is an allowed source stratum and is the appropriate stratum for cosmology. The exact historical source form/citation still needs a source-level etymological record before `kosma-` is claimed as historically derived rather than inspired.

Recommended lexical analysis:
- bound/root candidate: `kosma-` — cosmos/world-order, **Greek-inspired; source citation pending**
- mantle form: `kosma + thra` → `Kosmathra`
- literal project sense: **one who bears/embodies the mantle of the cosmos**
- poetic project sense: **cosmic soul-bearer / embodiment of the living cosmos**
- canon status: **MORPHOLOGICALLY VALID; ROOT ETYMOLOGY PROVISIONAL**

This makes “build the soul of the cosmos” poetic rather than a word-for-word gloss: to build Kosmathra is to participate in making/bearing the cosmos as a living mantle.

## Motto grammar

The two-word inscription is best treated as a **ritual motto / compressed poetic imperative**, not a fully explicit ordinary prose sentence.

**THARAVEL · KOSMATHRA**

Interpretive expansion:
> Go beyond through passionate living; help bring forth the cosmos as a living mantle.

English project gloss remains:
> **Go further · Live passionately · Build the soul of the cosmos**

If a future ordinary-prose Aureglossa translation is needed, it should be constructed from audited canonical verbs for “build/create,” object marking/order, and the desired noun phrase rather than pretending this two-word motto encodes every English word literally.

## Canon decision

1. Keep **Tharavel Kosmathra** as the adopted Archon Soulings / Living Nexus motto.
2. Retract the earlier root claims `thara = beyond`, `vel = vital fire`, and `-thra = soul/essence`.
3. Preserve older `thara = center` and `vel = steady` records until the 827-entry legacy lexicon is fully audited.
4. Treat `Tharavel` as an atomic adopted lexeme pending etymological resolution.
5. Treat `Kosmathra` as valid mantle morphology, with `kosma-` Greek-inspired pending source citation.
6. Do not generate new words by stripping or recombining these proposed roots until that audit is complete.
