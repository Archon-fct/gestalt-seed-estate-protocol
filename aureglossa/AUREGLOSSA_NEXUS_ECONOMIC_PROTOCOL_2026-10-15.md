Aureglossa is treated as a **living protocol layer** inside the Archon Soulings / Living Nexus / Gestalt Seed ecosystem. The language is not merely visual ornament. Its recorded forms can drive interaction, learning, provenance, editions, collaboration and future economic utility while preserving the distinction between canon, historical layers, proposals and financial instruments.

## 2. Living Nexus behavior

Aureglossa should feel alive because state changes are legible.

**Design rule:** motion explains state; it does not pretend to be intelligence.

Flow:
1. A visitor encounters the letter lattice.
2. Touch/focus reveals the recorded name and phonetic value.
3. The visitor chooses whether to create or use a local Soul.
4. If a Soul exists, an explicit public-safe canon mark may be added locally.
5. A completed contribution may become a Stone only through the existing contribution/consent process.
6. Public provenance is recorded separately from private journals, Silver/Nox data, client information and credentials.
7. Return to the Living Nexus integrates only the state the visitor chose to keep.

No hidden psychological profiling is required for this flow.

## 3. Soul / Stone / Souls distinction

### Soul
A consent-created identity/provenance object. Personal Soul identity remains non-transferable in the established architecture.

Aureglossa may add descriptive canon marks such as:
- `AUREGLOSSA:A`;
- an alphabet-learning milestone;
- an approved public contribution attestation;
- a relationship to an Aureglossa edition or Stone.

These marks are not currency balances.

### Stone
A contribution/work/provenance object. An Aureglossa Stone can represent:
- a verified lexical proposal;
- an approved poem/chant/translation edition;
- a pronunciation recording;
- a lesson;
- a type/glyph design proposal;
- software extending the atlas;
- cited linguistic research.

Every new contribution begins **PROVISIONAL** until human stewardship promotes it.

### Souls
Souls remains the reserved fungible ecosystem-currency concept.

Aureglossa is allowed to supply **future utility semantics** to Souls, but this implementation activates no monetary token, reward schedule, redemption promise, liquidity, exchange listing, revenue share or investment claim.

Possible future utility, only after the existing legal/security/testnet gates:
- access to designated language experiences;
- recognition of approved ecosystem participation;
- non-governance participation rewards;
- creator-defined redemption for clearly specified goods/services where compliant.

Canon authority does not transfer to token holders.

## 6. Design logic

This implementation follows six principles:

1. **Source before spectacle.** Canonical data drives the visuals.
2. **Consent before identity.** A Soul is created only by explicit action.
3. **Provenance before speculation.** Contributions and editions are traceable before they are financialized.
4. **Utility before token.** The ecosystem should be useful with Souls still inactive.
5. **Revenue from deliverables.** Services, lessons, editions and licenses create value before market narratives.
6. **Human stewardship remains final.** AI systems can research, implement, test and propose; they do not silently acquire canon, ownership or wallet authority.
