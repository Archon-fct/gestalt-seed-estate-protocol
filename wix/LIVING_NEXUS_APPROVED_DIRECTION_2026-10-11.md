# Living Nexus — Owner-approved visual and experience direction (2026-10-11)

Status: APPROVED DIRECTION, NOT VISUAL ACCEPTANCE OR PUBLICATION. Additive to the canonical continuation contract, HUD base specification, touchstone checklist and preview release gates. No existing architecture or visual reference is superseded.

## Governing principle
Preserve the living auric six-world constellation, luminous central Nexus, translucent organic membranes, flowing filaments, dark negative space, restrained gold/teal/indigo/pearl/violet, canonical Aureglossa, responsive Living Thread, Stillness, immediate Silence and optional Atmosphere. Do not substitute a generic dashboard, cards, flat navigation, stock sci-fi HUD, or fantasy landscape. Professional clarity and conversion must coexist with the established arcane interface.

## Approved refinements
1. Nexus: central meeting field with meaningful, localized responses to hover, keyboard focus, touch and world selection; retain stable world positions and accessible labels. Maintain an immediately discoverable Book a Session route.
2. Worlds: preserve their actual controllers and routes. Aura Dynamics: layered human-form educational visualization, without claims of diagnostic aura measurement. Sessions: calmer Inner Sanctum and straightforward booking details. Workshops: existing Attend / Host / Invite / Collaborate convergence. Aureglossa: audited canonical word and glyph relationships with provenance. Gestalt: approved attribution and relationship graph. Journal/Stories: reading-first presentation with restrained motion. Living Threshold stays the contribution entrance, not a seventh primary world.
3. HUD: embedded primary controls; optional Guide Me, Search, Thread and atmosphere; contextual action surfaces. Mobile retains recognizable constellation and touch-friendly world selector rather than collapsing to generic cards.
4. Motion: restrained slow breathing, localized filaments, short selection pulses, membrane crossing and return. Never perpetual distracting spinning, fake scanning, or unrequested camera/audio. Reduced motion, Stillness, and Silence remain independent and functional.
5. Living Thread: session-local by default; opt-in save; Continue / Save / Let it dissolve must operate on real state. Never mix client-sensitive information into public visitor state.
6. Public Field Guide: grounded in approved public material with navigable sources, graceful non-AI fallback. Private Nox/Silver review and operations remain separately authenticated; exclude private bundles, prompts, client information, and credentials from public builds.
7. Preserve existing Wix booking, forms, contribution fallback, URLs and SEO; never replace live homepage or navigation during preview.

## Implementation order
A. Complete Nexus → Aura Dynamics → Sessions → existing Wix booking as a real end-to-end visitor journey.
B. Apply consistent shared material/motion/Thread/accessibility behavior across all six worlds and Threshold without rewriting controllers.
C. Add bounded public guidance and separate private review tools only after reliable public functionality.
