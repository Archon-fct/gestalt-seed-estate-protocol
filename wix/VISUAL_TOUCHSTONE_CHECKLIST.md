# Living Nexus — Visual Touchstone Acceptance Checklist

Status: RELEASE GATE

Use together with the two owner-approved Living Nexus visual touchstone images supplied in the project conversation.

This checklist does not authorize copying incidental image artifacts or decorative pseudo-language. It captures the structural qualities the implementation must preserve.

## Central field

- NEXUS / THE MEETING FIELD remains the compositional center.
- The center reads as luminous living intelligence, not a logo card or ordinary navigation hub.
- Multiple translucent auric membranes imply depth and breathing field behavior.
- THARAVEL ~ KOSMATHRA remains a governing inscription.

## Worlds

The six primary worlds remain spatially related around the Nexus:
- Aura Dynamics — The Living Field
- Aureglossa — The Living Tongue
- Sessions — The Inner Sanctum
- Gestalt — The Living Archive
- Workshops — The Convergence
- Journal / Stories — The Astral Archive

Each world should read as an embedded pocket membrane / dimension, not a rectangular card.

Worlds should differ subtly in resonance/palette while belonging to one organism.

## Relationships

- Filaments visibly relate worlds to the Nexus.
- Journey memory adds luminous seeds rather than badges or points.
- Search and Guide Me change emphasis/proximity/resonance of the constellation.
- Visited worlds retain a restrained trace.
- Returning from a dimension visibly integrates back into the field.

## Living Thread

- Living Thread is part of the field.
- A panel/drawer may provide accessible text and controls, but it must not become the dominant visual metaphor.
- Journey seeds form a temporary constellation.
- Continue / Save / Let it dissolve remain grounded and reachable.
- Let it dissolve genuinely clears the relevant local journey state.

## Soul

- Create My Soul is explicit opt-in.
- A created Soul changes the field/auric response rather than opening a crypto dashboard.
- Soul remains identity/provenance, separate from the Souls currency.
- No wallet is required for Soul v0.1.

## Controls

- Search, Guide Me, Stillness, Atmosphere/Silence, Field Journal and Soul controls remain subordinate to the environment.
- Controls are keyboard/touch accessible.
- Audio is opt-in; Silence is immediate.
- Reduced-motion users retain meaning without animation.

## Responsive health

### ~1280px
The full relational constellation should be immediately legible.

### ~820px iPad portrait
The composition may tighten/rebalance but must remain a constellation around the Meeting Field. Do not collapse into a dashboard.

### ~390px
The environment may use tighter geometry, progressive disclosure, or panning/overlays, but the Nexus/world relationship must remain perceptible. Do not become a generic vertical card list.

## Automatic failure conditions

Reject the build if:
- the main view reads primarily as cards/dashboard;
- the Nexus loses central authority;
- worlds become a flat menu;
- Living Thread becomes primarily a conventional app sidebar;
- visual decoration overwhelms usable interaction;
- touchstone resemblance is achieved by static fantasy artwork while the interface itself is not alive;
- responsive behavior destroys the constellation metaphor;
- canonical Aureglossa is replaced by invented glyph decoration;
- Silver/private material appears.
