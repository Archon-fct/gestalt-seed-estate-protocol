# Security and Authority Boundaries

## Never store here
- private keys
- wallet seed/recovery phrases
- passwords
- API secrets
- authentication tokens
- private personal information

## Signing
No AI identity in this repository has autonomous blockchain signing authority. Transactions that spend funds, move assets, grant approvals, deploy contracts, or change wallet permissions require explicit authorized human signing.

## Canon
AI systems may propose material. They do not independently promote material to Gestalt Seed canon.

## Rights
A blockchain token is not automatically the underlying copyright or license. Work, file, edition, NFT, license, and copyright are separate records.

## Untrusted contributions
External submissions are untrusted until reviewed. Future direct-upload systems should include size/type limits, malware scanning, abuse controls, privacy/retention rules, and human moderation.

## Darchon.nft
Darchon.nft is an identity/provenance root. It is not blanket ownership of every Gestalt Seed asset and does not itself grant transaction authority.
